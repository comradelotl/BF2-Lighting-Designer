Ambient dome — single veil gradient per aerosol × sun

For each CAMS aerosol×sun a single 0→10 haze gradient is grown, seeded at the clear dome, per haze step taking the most chromatic dome colour that is a close neighbour, smoothed to keep lightness steady. The haze 0→0.5 span is perceptually smoothed (OKLab midpoint). The mostly white glare lobe (forward-scatter band at the solar elevation, hatched on the map) is thrown out for it to not distort the overal image. Two variants: the most-chromatic seed curve with lightness ramping (hue-only at haze 0 → true dome lightness at haze 10), and a hue-only curve with lightness flattened to the haze-0 dome. The hue-only lightness flattened variant was ported to the model. All colours precomputed in sky_data.js; this page holds no tables.
