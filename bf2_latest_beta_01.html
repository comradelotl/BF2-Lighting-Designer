BF2 Lighting Designer

1.) Pick the ground color of the map (sand, grass, etc.).
2.) Then input the Lightmanager.sunDirection y-value from the sky.con to receive the current sun angle. Dial in the time of day according to this and according to the sky texture.
3.) Tune the rest to your liking and how it fits best. Copy-paste the results into the level's sky.con
